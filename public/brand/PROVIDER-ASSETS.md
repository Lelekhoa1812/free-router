# Provider logo assets

These local image assets are used only to identify upstream providers in the FreeRouter control plane. Provider names remain visible beside the marks; the logos do not imply endorsement or a partnership.

- `google-ai-studio.png` — Google AI Studio mark from Google's live product page and official Google-hosted product logo: <https://aistudio.google.com/> and <https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/ai_studio/v1/web-96dp/logo_ai_studio_color_1x_web_96dp.png>.
- `nvidia.svg` — Full NVIDIA horizontal lockup from NVIDIA's official BioNeMo repository: <https://github.com/NVIDIA/bionemo-framework/blob/main/docs/docs/assets/images/logo-white.svg>. The original white-and-green version is retained for the dark dashboard; the complete lockup keeps the eye with its wordmark, consistent with [NVIDIA's logo guidance](https://www.nvidia.com/en-gb/about-nvidia/legal-info/logo-brand-usage/).
- `openrouter.svg` — OpenRouter's official Volt glyph SVG, downloaded from the OpenRouter brand assets page: <https://openrouter.ai/brand>.

FreeRouter's own mark in `freerouter-mark.svg` and `/icon.svg` is original and depicts multiple routes converging through a gateway.
